# LiturgyFlow — External Repertoire List for Rights Discussion

**Purpose:** Transparent starting inventory of hymn/song titles currently available in LiturgyFlow, so a music publisher / licensing partner can identify repertoire they may represent and discuss permissions.

**Prepared for:** Potential music publisher / licensing partner discussion  
**Product:** LiturgyFlow

## Important disclaimers

- LiturgyFlow does **not** claim ownership of third-party copyrighted songs or lyrics.
- This inventory reflects **what is listed in LiturgyFlow’s current repertoire**, not a determination of legal ownership.
- **No copyright ownership has been guessed.** Empty publisher/copyright fields mean that information is not recorded in LiturgyFlow.
- Composer/author names are shown **exactly as attributed in LiturgyFlow**, where available.
- No separate lyricist, publisher, copyright notice, rights-origin, or public-domain designation is currently recorded for these items.
- Classification **C (Public domain)** is **not used** for any item: LiturgyFlow does not explicitly mark any song as public domain, and this document does not infer public-domain status.

## Rights classification key

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **A** | Rights information identified (publisher/copyright notice recorded) |
| **B** | Rights holder / publisher needs verification (composer/author attributed, but no confirmed rights holder/publisher) |
| **C** | Public domain / likely public domain (only if explicitly identified or strong project evidence) |
| **D** | Unknown (no composer/author, publisher, or copyright information recorded) |
